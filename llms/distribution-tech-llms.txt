# distribution.tech

> Tell your agent what you're shipping: it finds the X creators your buyers already follow and brings back proof. One buy, one approval, proof for every post.

Live today: AI-built plans from one chat, made of reviewed X creators, each with an all-in price per post, quote or thread, that you approve and pay for online; your brand workspace with plans, approvals, proof and results; campaigns our team runs end to end, creator relationships and payouts included; an agent in your workspace, the plan chat, that drafts a priced plan, finds creators and changes the plan, acting only after you confirm each change; and agent access, where your agents read campaign status, proof and results and start and approve plans inside spending limits a person sets.

Every page also serves a markdown version at its URL plus ".md" (for example https://distribution.tech/how-it-works.md).

## For agents

A brand's own agents use its workspace with an API key the brand creates at https://app.distribution.tech/workspace/developers (sent as Authorization: Bearer <key>). Read scopes cover network coverage, approved creators on sale, audience fit, campaigns, plan quotes, proof and results of paid posts, and company context. The write scopes campaigns:write and plans:approve let an agent start a campaign from a goal, add or remove creators on its plan, replace the guidelines every booked creator reads, and approve a plan for a payment link. Anything that spends runs only inside a spending mandate a person sets on the key in the workspace: the most per plan, the most per month, the channels, whether the key may approve, an expiry, and brand terms a person accepted. Approving needs the mandate to allow it, a request over the per-plan or monthly cap is refused before anything is spent, and every write that changes a campaign or plan needs an Idempotency-Key, so a retry never runs twice. Any valid key can also report a bug, gap or idea with feedback_send (POST /feedback) and list the brand's reports with feedback_list (GET /feedback: full detail for this key's reports, a summary for other keys'); these need no scope and no Idempotency-Key, and the same report from the same key within 15 minutes is kept once.
